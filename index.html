The house

A haven in the old town

We opened HAVEN to slow things down. Coffee roasted within the valley, bread proved overnight, eggs from a farm up the road — served in a light-filled room of lime-washed walls and worn oak.

Come for a flat white and the morning paper, stay for brunch that runs until the afternoon light moves off the canal.
