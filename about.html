Our story

Built on slow mornings

From a six-seat coffee bar to the brunch room locals queue for on Sundays.

The first lever machine
2016 — the beginning

From a corner counter

HAVEN started as a narrow coffee counter on Rue Sainte-Claire with one lever machine, a single pastry case and a stubborn belief that mornings shouldn't be rushed.

Word travelled. The counter became a kitchen, the kitchen became a room, and the room became the place you bring people the first time they visit Annecy.

The market run, 7am
How we cook

Led by the market

Every plate begins at the morning market on the canal. We buy what is best that day and write the specials on the way back.

It means the menu is never quite the same twice — and that's exactly the point.

What we stand for

Three things we won't cut corners on

☕

Coffee with provenance

Single-origin, roasted weekly in the valley, and dialled in fresh every morning before doors open.

🤝

People over speed

We'd rather you wait two minutes longer than rush a flat white or hurry you out of your seat.

♻️

As little waste as we can

Trim becomes stock, day-old bread becomes the French toast, and what's left is composted with a neighbour's farm.
