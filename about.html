Our story

Not Heaven, but pretty close!

From a six-seat coffee bar to the all-day brunch room Bengaluru queues for on Sundays.

The first lever machine
2016 — the beginning

From a corner counter

Atul's Cafekery started as a narrow coffee counter in Koramangala with one lever machine, a single pastry case and a stubborn belief that mornings should be colourful, generous and unhurried.

Word travelled. The counter became a kitchen, the kitchen became a room, and the room became the place you bring people the first time they visit the neighbourhood.

The market run, 7am
How we cook

Led by the market

Every plate begins at the morning market down the road. We buy what is best that day and write the specials on the way back.

It means the menu is never quite the same twice — and that's exactly the point. Every season brings its own brunch.
